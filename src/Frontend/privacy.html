Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

Account details (name, email), authentication tokens, problem submissions (code, errors, logs you choose to share), usage metrics for plan limits, and support messages.

How we use data

To authenticate you, run AI debugging, generate educational articles, enforce plan quotas, improve reliability, and provide support (including Telegram account restore flows).

AI providers

Problem content may be sent to configured LLM providers (for example Gemini, Groq, NVIDIA, OpenRouter) to generate debugging analysis. Avoid submitting secrets you do not want processed by third parties.

Soft delete

Account deletion is soft-delete by design. Deleted accounts stop consuming free-plan abuse loops; recovery may be available through support with email OTP verification.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact the DevPilot AI support channels listed on the Community page.
